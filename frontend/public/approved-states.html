Frequently Asked Questions

Which commercial vehicles require AIS 140 GPS in India?
As per MoRTH mandate, AIS 140 GPS is compulsory for: All School Buses, Passenger Taxis / Cabs (Ola, Uber, Tourist), Commercial Trucks & Tankers, Mining Dumpers, Hazardous Goods Carriers, and Interstate Buses.
How to get an AIS 140 RTO Fitness Certificate?
Arshi Enterprises provides doorstep installation. After installing the certified device, our team uploads the IMEI and vehicle chassis details to the State Transport Vahan Portal and provides the official passing certificate for your RTO inspection.
Do you provide SIM card and recharge?
Yes! Every AIS 140 device comes with 1-Year or 2-Year pre-activated Government approved dual eSIM with unlimited pan-India data roaming.
